Acme Corp was founded in 2020 by Jane Smith.
The company is headquartered in Vancouver, Canada.
Acme Corp builds AI-powered tools for developers.
The company has 50 employees as of 2024.
The CEO is Jane Smith and the CTO is Bob Johnson.
Acme Corp's main product is called CodeHelper.
CodeHelper costs $29 per month for individuals.
Enterprise pricing starts at $500 per month.
